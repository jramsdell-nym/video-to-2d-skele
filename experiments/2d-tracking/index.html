MediaPipe configuration and interpretation

MediaPipe 1.2.2; official float16 Full and Heavy model bundles, with saved SHA256 hashes. VIDEO mode, CPU delegate, up to four poses, detection/presence/tracking thresholds 0.5. All 120 frames processed sequentially per clip using source-derived millisecond timestamps. A fresh tracker is created per clip and independently checked against its first-frame replay.

The same 17 named joints feed hitter association; the default overlay and metrics use 12 shared joints. Full-33 mode adds face, hand, heel and foot points only for MediaPipe. Estimated hip-centered world coordinates are saved separately and are not used in this 2D comparison. No calibrated 3D or KinaTrax accuracy claim is made.

The multi-pose path keeps temporal ROI tracking but does not enable MediaPipe’s single-pose landmark-smoothing filter. No additional smoothing, guessed gap filling or dropped live-stream frames. Apparent steadiness does not prove accuracy or rule out lag. These clips have no manual 2D ground truth.

Methods, runtime, provenance and limitations

Apple Vision revision 1, unchanged upright ≤960px preprocessing. Candidate selection initializes on the largest visible person and gates later matches by overlap, center motion and size. H1 is a local track label, not biometric identity. YOLO11s-pose uses COCO's 17 anatomical joints with the same selector; the common 12 limb joints are drawn. A confidence score is not a guarantee of correct localization.

Bat A: YOLO11m, COCO baseball-bat class 34, 960px input, confidence ≥0.25 and a 50px box-to-wrist distance gate. The gate was tightened after seeing rack-bat false positives on these same clips. Bbox detections only; box corners and wrist positions are not assigned as physical endpoints. Small dots show recent observed box centers. Bat B: OpenCV Line Segment Detector, hand proximity and frame-difference support, with at most two frames of Lucas–Kanade propagation gated by forward/backward error, robust affine fit and current image edges. The line can still lock onto arms, rack bats, poles or nets. Segment endpoints carry no tip/knob identity.

All inference runs locally. Learned models: official public YOLO11 weights, AGPL-3.0 / Enterprise licensing. OpenCV: Apache 2.0. Model hashes and the exact package lock are saved. Mac timings are measured on an Apple M3 Pro; they are not iPhone benchmarks. See the report for the proposed mobile path.
